# Google Play submission checklist — BillNexa v1.9

## Suggested listing
Name: BillNexa: GST Invoice Maker
Short description: Create GST bills, manage products and export print-ready PDF or HD JPG.
Category: Business
Developer: use the verified name in your Play Console account.
Support email: business@pixelfirestudio.in (confirm it works).

Full description:
BillNexa is an offline billing and invoice studio for shops, small businesses and freelancers. Create GST and non-GST invoices, maintain product and customer records, enter HSN/SAC, discounts and tax rates, and record paid or outstanding balances. Export premium saffron invoices as print-ready PDF or high-resolution JPG, share files through Android and keep local JSON backups. No sign-in required. Tax calculations are based on user-entered values and are not a guarantee of compliance.

## App content draft (verify against the signed final bundle)
- Ads: No, current build has no advertising SDK.
- App access: all functionality is available without sign-in.
- Data safety: records are processed and stored on-device, no developer server or analytics. User-initiated exports/shares use chosen apps. Review Play's definitions and exclusions before declaring no collection/sharing.
- Privacy policy: publish docs/privacy.html as a publicly accessible web page, verify URL and contact. Terms of Use and Help & Support are available in the app and in docs/terms.html and docs/support.html.
- Account deletion: no accounts are created; local deletion is available under More → Privacy & data controls.
- Financial features: declare applicable features accurately; this app records invoice/payment amounts but does not process payments, provide loans or hold funds.
- Content rating, target audience and declarations: complete the Play Console questionnaires honestly.
- App icon: upload a 512×512 Play listing icon; in-app adaptive launcher icon is separate.
- Feature graphic: 1024×500. Upload real app screenshots (no customer personal information).
- Signed AAB: version code 10, target API 36; upload-key signing required.
- Test: invoice totals, GST modes, old database upgrade, product search/add, PDF and JPG output, long invoices, file sharing, print, backup/restore, local deletion, Android 16 layout and keyboard behavior.
- Verify Play pre-launch report and fix issues. If the account is subject to new personal-account testing requirements, complete the required closed test before applying for production access.
- No approval or successful production release is guaranteed.
